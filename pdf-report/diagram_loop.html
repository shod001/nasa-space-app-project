Daily Mission Loop — repeated across the 14-day / Sol mission
1
Morning Briefing
A.S.T.R.O., the AI copilot, greets the commander and sets up today’s challenge.
2
NASA Data Event
A live DONKI space-weather feed or a scripted card arrives with a real-data badge.
3
Power Allocation
Energy cubes are dragged between Life Support, Shields, Science Lab and Greenhouse.
4
Decision Card
Two or three options — safe, balanced or risky — each with visible trade-offs.
5
Day Resolution
Power, oxygen, food, shields, crew health, morale and science totals are updated.
6
Debrief & Badges
At mission end, A.S.T.R.O. reviews the run and up to 7 merit badges are awarded.
↻ Steps 1–5 repeat every in-game day until Sol / Day 14 or an early mission return.
Step 6 triggers once, at mission end.
